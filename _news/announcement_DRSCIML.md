<b>DRSCIML 2025</b>

We hosted an international workshop on the foundations of scientific-ML, [DRSCIML 2025](https://drsciml.github.io/drsciml/). With Prof. Jakob Zech at the University of Heidelberg, we had secured a grant which was used to host this event at The University of Manchester on 9th and 10th September 2025.
We hosted plenary speakers from multiple countries, Prof. Olga Fink (EPFL), Prof. Olga Mula (University of Vienna), Prof. Ricardo Vinuesa (University of Michigan) and Dr. George Stepaniants (Caltech). There were 10 other speakers from 6 countries.
