# Consultancy & Advisory

> Applying Foundational Artificial Intelligence Research for Practical Scientific Challenges.

I seek to engage with organisations seeking independent, research-informed advice on complex scientific problems which can typically leverage ML. Most typical cases where my efforts of this type are best suited are if there is a need to teach modern ML models (transformers, LLMs, neural operators and PINNs) and/or to design them specifically for solving for the time-evolution of a high-dimensional system.

Dynamical systems that we currently think about are generally from the realm of (multi-)physics: those involving a flowing fluid - like ocean waves or blood flow in the arteries, time evolution of lattice models of magnetism and chemical reactions - to name a few of our active application areas. *As a group specializing in the foundations of AI, we leverage the key insight that all such systems can be dealt with by fairly universal transformer based ML systems, and hence experiences and learnings from one transfer to the others.*
